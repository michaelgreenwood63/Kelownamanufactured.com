Sierra's Westside

2440 Old Okanagan Highway, West Kelowna — 53 units, predominantly Westbank First Nation land.

WFN Land*

The short version: Sierra's Westside (also spelled "Sierras" on some listings) is a 53-unit all-ages community on Old Okanagan Highway. The great majority of listings we've reviewed confirm it sits on Westbank First Nation land — but one listing showed a conflicting "Other Lease" status rather than "Indigenous Lands." See the land status note below before assuming every lot is treated identically.

Pad rent and pets

Pad rent runs around $580/month across multiple listings. Two dogs are allowed with park approval (no dangerous breeds), and cats are permitted with restrictions. This is an all-ages community, not age-restricted.

Lease term

The WFN headlease for this park runs to December 30, 2089.

⚠️ A land status note worth reading

Five of six recent listings we reviewed for this park explicitly confirm Westbank First Nation land status. One listing, however, showed a different title type ("Other Lease") and explicitly marked the property as not on Indigenous land. This could reflect a data-entry inconsistency on that one listing, or it could mean the park's parcels aren't entirely uniform — similar to how Westgate is split into two differently-structured halves. If you're looking at a specific unit here, it's worth confirming that unit's exact legal description rather than assuming the park-wide pattern applies automatically. We're happy to help you check.

This page provides general information based on publicly available sources, land registry records, and current MLS listing data. It is not legal advice. Pad rent, policies, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.
