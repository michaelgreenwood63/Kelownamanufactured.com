Trail Park Mobile Villas

1699 Ross Road, West Kelowna (Lakeview Heights)

BC Land — Confirmed

The short version: Trail Park Mobile Villas is an all-ages manufactured home community in West Kelowna's Lakeview Heights neighbourhood — a regular residential area, not a First Nations reserve.

What we know

Because Trail Park sits on ordinary BC land, it falls under the province's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act like Crystal Springs and Pinewoods Villa nearby — not Westbank First Nation's Residential Premises Law, which governs most of the parks along the Boucherie Road corridor a few minutes away. Pets are allowed subject to the park's own restrictions. This is an all-ages community, unlike many of the 45+/55+ parks in the area.

We don't yet have confirmed pad rent or pet-policy numbers for this specific park — worth asking the park directly before you make an offer.

Is Trail Park Mobile Villas on reserve land?

Is this an age-restricted community?
No, Trail Park Mobile Villas is all-ages, unlike many nearby 45+ or 55+ parks.
