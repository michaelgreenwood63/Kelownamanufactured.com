Westview Village

1999 Highway 97 South — 276 units, 18+, on Westbank First Nation land. The largest park in our directory.

WFN Land

The short version: Westview Village is the largest manufactured home community we've mapped anywhere in the Central Okanagan — 276 units, managed by Compass Communities, on a 125-year Westbank First Nation lease running to February 2151. One detail here matters more than almost anywhere else in our directory: the park holds a contractual Right of First Refusal on resale.

Pad rent and sections

Pad rent ranges from about $620 to $700/month depending on which part of the park a unit is in — Westview has a distinct, lower-priced "family section" alongside its standard 18+ section.

Age restriction

18+.

What "Right of First Refusal" actually means for sellers

Westview Village's lease gives the park itself the right to buy a home back before it can be sold to an outside buyer, on the same terms you've negotiated with your buyer. In practice, this means: once you have an accepted offer, the park gets a window to match it and take the home themselves instead of letting the sale to your buyer proceed. This is an unusual provision — most parks in our directory don't have it — and it's genuinely important to understand and plan around before listing a home here, not something to discover partway through a sale.

Pets

Up to 2 dogs or 2 cats are allowed, no vicious breeds.

Land status

Westview is on Westbank First Nation land under a 125-year lease with Compass Communities. See our full jurisdiction breakdown.

Common Questions

Westview Village — FAQ

How big is Westview Village?
276 units — the largest manufactured home park in our entire Central Okanagan directory.
What does the park's Right of First Refusal mean for me as a seller?
It means the park itself has the right to buy your home back on the same terms as an accepted offer from an outside buyer, before that sale can proceed. It's an unusual provision worth planning around before you list, not discovering partway through a sale.
What's the age restriction?
18+.
Is there a cheaper section of the park?
Yes — Westview has a distinct, lower-priced 'family section' alongside its standard 18+ section, with pad rent around $620/month there versus up to $700/month elsewhere in the park.
How long does the lease run?
A 125-year lease with Compass Communities, running to February 12, 2151.
