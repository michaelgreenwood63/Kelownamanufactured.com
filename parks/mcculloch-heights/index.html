McCulloch Heights

4075 McClain Road, SE Kelowna — the second of two manufactured home parks we've confirmed in this part of the city.

The short version: McCulloch Heights is a manufactured home park on McClain Road in South East Kelowna, on regular BC provincial land, with pad rent around $507-525 a month plus separately billed quarterly water/sewer.

Land & Jurisdiction

McCulloch Heights sits on regular BC provincial/municipal land, not a First Nations reserve. BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies as normal — see our full land jurisdiction guide for how this compares to the reserve-land parks elsewhere in our directory.

Pad Rent & Utilities

Pad rent runs roughly $507 to $525 a month, with new owners generally landing at the higher end. Water and sewer are billed separately on a quarterly basis, at roughly $201 per quarter based on what we've seen — budget for this on top of the pad rent itself, since it's not bundled in.

Pets

Pets are approved individually, one per unit, with a specific restriction that dogs can't be taller than knee height. This is a case-by-case approval process rather than a blanket yes or no — worth confirming directly with the park manager for your specific pet before assuming it'll be approved.

This page provides general information based on publicly available land registry and MLS data, and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet, and rental policies can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park before relying on them.

Common Questions

McCulloch Heights — FAQ

How much is pad rent at McCulloch Heights?
Roughly $507 to $525 a month, with new owners typically at the higher end of that range. Water and sewer are billed separately, roughly $201 per quarter.
Can I bring a dog to McCulloch Heights?
Pets are approved individually, one per unit, with dogs restricted to knee height or under. Confirm with the park manager for your specific dog before assuming approval.
Is McCulloch Heights on reserve land?
No — it's on regular BC provincial/municipal land, so BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies.
Is there an age restriction at McCulloch Heights?
We have no confirmed age restriction for this park — treat it as all-ages unless you confirm otherwise with the park directly.
