Trepanier Creek Mobile Home Park

5432 Chidley Road, Peachland — a 55+ community with a no-rentals policy.

The short version: Trepanier Creek Mobile Home Park is a 55+ community on Chidley Road in Peachland, on regular BC provincial land, with pad rent around $589 a month.

Land & Jurisdiction

Trepanier Creek sits on regular BC provincial/municipal land, not a First Nations reserve. BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies as normal — see our full land jurisdiction guide for context.

Age Restriction, Pad Rent & Pets

Trepanier Creek is a 55+ community with pad rent around $589 a month. One small dog (up to 20 lbs) or one cat is allowed with park approval.

Rentals

Rentals are not allowed at Trepanier Creek — if you're buying as an investment property rather than to live in, this isn't the right community.

This page provides general information based on publicly available land registry and MLS data, and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet, and rental policies can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park before relying on them.
