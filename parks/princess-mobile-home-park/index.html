Princess Mobile Home Park

2065 Boucherie Road, West Kelowna — 90 units on Westbank First Nation land.

WFN Land

The short version: Princess Mobile Home Park is a 90-unit community on Westbank First Nation land along the Boucherie Road corridor. Like most WFN parks, there's no registered headlease on file — homeowners deal directly with the park operator (Princess Enterprises) rather than a formally registered long-term lease. See our full breakdown of WFN land for what that means generally.

Pets and rentals

Princess does not allow dogs, but cats are permitted. Rentals are not allowed — this is a park for owner-occupants.

Buying in

At least one recent listing noted a minimum credit score around 730 for park approval — worth confirming directly with the park manager before you're deep into financing a specific unit, since approval requirements like this can affect your timeline.

Land status

Princess sits on Westbank First Nation land, governed by WFN's own Residential Premises Law rather than BC's provincial Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act.

Common Questions

Princess Mobile Home Park — FAQ

Does Princess Mobile Home Park allow dogs?
No. Cats are allowed, but dogs are not permitted at this park.
Can I rent out a home in Princess Mobile Home Park?
No — the park does not allow rentals, including to tenants who aren't the owner.
Is there a credit score requirement to buy here?
At least one recent listing referenced a minimum credit score around 730 for park approval. Confirm the current requirement directly with the park manager before relying on it.
What law governs this park?
Princess sits on Westbank First Nation land, so WFN's own Residential Premises Law applies, not BC's provincial Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. See our full land-jurisdiction explainer for details.
