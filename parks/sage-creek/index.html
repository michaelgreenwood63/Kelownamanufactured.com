Sage Creek

2084 Elk Road, West Kelowna — a 45+ community on Westbank First Nation land.

WFN Land

The short version: Sage Creek is a well-established 45+ manufactured home community on Westbank First Nation land, built in prefabricated ranch-style homes on permanent foundations rather than the older single/double-wide style.

Pad rent and what it covers

Pad rent for new owners runs around $625/month. Garbage collection is billed separately by a private company, at roughly $486/year, rather than being bundled into the pad rent. Some listings note the fee also provides access to a clubhouse, fitness area, and gated security.

Lease term

Sage Creek operates under two separate Westbank First Nation headleases: an original 99-year lease from 2006 (expiring 2105 — confirmed by listings noting "79 years remaining" as of 2026), and a second 125-year lease from 2018 to 2143 covering a newer parcel added to the community, for which the operator (Sage Creek Developments) paid $600,000 upfront rather than ongoing rent. Either way, your own pad tenancy is month-to-month regardless of how much headlease term remains.

Pets and rentals

Up to 2 pets are allowed with management approval, dogs of any size, though vicious breeds are excluded. Rentals are explicitly not allowed — multiple listings state this directly: "WFN Land, monthly lease. NO RENTALS ALLOWED."

Age restriction

45+.

Common Questions

Sage Creek — FAQ

What's the pad rent at Sage Creek?
Around $625/month for new owners, as of recent listings. Garbage collection (~$486/year) is billed separately by a private company, not included in the pad rent.
Can I rent out my home in Sage Creek?
No. Multiple current listings state directly that rentals are not allowed under the park's WFN land lease.
How much lease term is left at Sage Creek?
The original 99-year headlease (from 2006) runs to 2105 — about 79 years remaining as of 2026. A newer section of the park operates under a separate 125-year lease running to 2143.
Is there an age restriction at Sage Creek?
Yes, it's a 45+ community.
What pets are allowed?
Up to 2 pets with management approval, any size, but vicious breeds are excluded.
