Jubilee

2005 Boucherie Road, West Kelowna — 145 units on Westbank First Nation land

Westbank First Nation Land

Jubilee is a 145-unit manufactured home community at 2005 Boucherie Road in West Kelowna, on Westbank First Nation land. It's one of the larger parks in our directory, with a headlease running to May 19, 2104.

Jubilee has two policies worth knowing before you shop here: no dogs are permitted at all, and rentals are not allowed. If either of those matters to you, this isn't the right community.

Financing is also a real consideration at Jubilee specifically — lending is limited, with Peace Hills Trust essentially the only lender willing to finance a home here. This is a recurring pattern across several WFN-land parks; see our financing guide for why leasehold reserve-land homes are harder to finance than a typical home, and plan your mortgage pre-approval around this before making an offer.

This page provides general information only and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.
