Kelowna West Estates

610 Highway 97 South — 70 units on Westbank First Nation land

Westbank First Nation Land

Kelowna West Estates is a 70-unit manufactured home community at 610 Highway 97 South, on Westbank First Nation land, with a headlease running to March 13, 2057.

One address note: the park's own registered address is 610 Highway 97 South, though its management company's office is located at 669 Katherine Road nearby — if you've seen this park referred to by a Katherine Road address, that's the management office, not the park entrance itself.

Pets require park approval before moving in — it's not an automatic yes, so check with management about a specific pet before counting on bringing it. Rentals are not permitted at this park.

This page provides general information only and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.

Common Questions

Kelowna West Estates — FAQ

Is Kelowna West Estates on Katherine Road or Highway 97?
The park's own registered address is 610 Highway 97 South. Its management company's office is at 669 Katherine Road — a separate address that sometimes gets confused with the park entrance itself.
Is Kelowna West Estates on Westbank First Nation land?
Yes, confirmed on WFN land, governed by WFN's Residential Premises Law.
Can I rent out my home at Kelowna West Estates?
No, rentals are not permitted at this park.
Do I need approval to have a pet at Kelowna West Estates?
Yes — pets require park approval before you move in.
