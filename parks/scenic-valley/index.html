Scenic Valley

580 Highway 97 South, West Kelowna — a small 23-unit community on Westbank First Nation land.

WFN Land

The short version: Scenic Valley is one of the smallest manufactured home parks we've mapped in West Kelowna — 23 units, on Westbank First Nation land under a headlease running to July 2055.

What we know so far

This is a smaller, lower-profile park with less public listing activity than the larger WFN communities nearby, so we have less detail on its current pet policy and rental rules than we do for parks like Sage Creek or Westview Village. What's confirmed: 23 units, and a WFN headlease running to July 2055.

Land status

Scenic Valley sits on Westbank First Nation land. WFN's own Residential Premises Law governs tenancies here, not BC's provincial Act — see our full explainer.

We'd rather tell you plainly that our current data on this park is thinner than most, rather than guess at pet or rental policy. If you're looking at a specific unit here, get in touch and we'll dig into the current rules directly.
