Pinewoods Villa

1750 Lenz Road, West Kelowna

Freehold — You Own the Land

The short version: Pinewoods Villa is one of the only manufactured home communities in our whole directory where you actually own your land, rather than paying pad rent to a park operator. It's set up as freehold ownership with an HOA (homeowners' association), with a modest HOA fee covering shared costs instead of a monthly lease payment to anyone.

Why this is a fundamentally different kind of purchase

Every other park in our directory — whether on Westbank First Nation land, Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7, or regular BC provincial land — involves paying pad rent to lease the ground your home sits on. Pinewoods Villa doesn't work that way: you hold title to your own parcel, the same way you would with a detached house. That has real practical consequences — no pad rent increases to track, no park-operator consent needed to sell, no lease expiry decades down the road to think about. See our full explanation of freehold vs. leasehold for how this compares to every other kind of park in this market.

What it actually costs, and who it's for

Instead of pad rent, owners pay an HOA fee of around $85 a month — covering shared costs like road maintenance, not land use. The community is age-restricted 55+. Dogs are allowed, and rentals are permitted with HOA approval (a homeowners'-association process, not a landlord's discretion the way it works in a leasehold park).

Why we flag this one specifically: if long-term security and straightforward financing matter more to you than price, a freehold community like this is worth comparing directly against a leasehold park before you decide — even where the sale price looks similar, what you're actually buying is very different.

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. Park policies, pad rent, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.

Common Questions

Pinewoods Villa — FAQ

Do I own the land at Pinewoods Villa, or just the home?
You own the land. You hold freehold title to your own parcel, with an HOA (not a strata corporation) handling shared costs, rather than leasing a pad from a park operator — a fundamentally different (and generally more secure) ownership structure than any leasehold manufactured home park.
What does the $85/month cover if it's not pad rent?
It's an HOA fee covering shared costs like road maintenance for the community — not a land lease payment to anyone.
Is Pinewoods Villa age-restricted?
Yes, 55+.
Does owning the land make financing easier?
Generally, yes — lenders are typically more comfortable with freehold land than leasehold pads, especially compared to parks on reserve land where only specific lenders will finance at all. Confirm current lending options with a mortgage broker for your specific situation.
