Westgate

1880 Old Boucherie Road, West Kelowna — 140 units, 55+, on Westbank First Nation land.

WFN Land

The short version: Westgate is a 140-unit, 55+ community with an unusual structure — it's effectively split into two halves with different leases, different management, and different financing rules. Knowing which half a specific unit is in matters a lot here.

The two-tier structure

The southwest 10 acres (units 1-70) are managed by Bayside Property Management, operate under a lease running to July 2036, and are cash-only — no financing available for these units. The northeast 10 acres have no registered headlease at all, and are managed separately by Princess Enterprises.

Financing

Limited lending is available through Peace Hills Trust on parts of this park. At least one listing referenced a minimum 730 credit score for park approval. Given the cash-only requirement on the SW half specifically, financing availability is genuinely one of the first things to confirm before you get attached to a specific unit here.

Pets and rentals

This is generally a no-pets, no-rentals park, though we've seen some indication that rules may vary slightly by unit or by how old that unit's specific tenancy agreement is — worth confirming current policy directly rather than assuming uniformity.

Westgate sits on Westbank First Nation land. See our full jurisdiction breakdown.

Common Questions

Westgate — FAQ

Why does Westgate have two different sets of rules?
The park is split into two separately-managed halves on 10 acres each. The southwest half (units 1-70) is cash-only with a lease to 2036, managed by Bayside Property Management. The northeast half has no registered lease and is managed separately by Princess Enterprises.
Can I get a mortgage for a home at Westgate?
It depends which half of the park the unit is in. The southwest half (units 1-70) is cash-only, no financing available. Limited lending through Peace Hills Trust may be available elsewhere in the park — confirm for the specific unit before assuming.
Is there an age restriction?
Yes, 55+.
Are pets allowed at Westgate?
Generally no, though pet policy may vary somewhat by unit or tenancy-agreement era — confirm current rules for the specific unit directly.
