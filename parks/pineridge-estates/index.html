Pineridge Estates

1860 Boucherie Road, West Kelowna — 132 units on Westbank First Nation land

Westbank First Nation Land

Pineridge Estates is a 132-unit manufactured home community at 1860 Boucherie Road in West Kelowna, on Westbank First Nation land. Like Pine Villa and Golden Homes nearby, Pineridge Estates does not have a formal headlease registered on file.

Pets require park approval, and rentals are not permitted. Pineridge is managed by the same company (RMD Group) that manages Golden Homes and Pine Villa — all three sit along the same stretch of Boucherie Road.

This page provides general information only and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.
