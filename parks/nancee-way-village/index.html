Nancee Way Village

1835 Westside Road, Kelowna — all-ages community, predominantly Westbank First Nation land

Westbank First Nation Land

Nancee Way Village is an all-ages manufactured home community at 1835 Westside Road in Kelowna. Most of the records we've reviewed confirm this park sits on Westbank First Nation land, with two headleases — one covering the northern 8 acres running to June 30, 2109, and another covering the southern portion running to September 5, 2118.

Worth confirming before you buy: the large majority of listings and official records we've reviewed for Nancee Way Village confirm WFN land status, but we've seen at least one specific unit's listing show a different zoning code and non-reserve status. This could reflect a data entry inconsistency, or it could mean this park's parcels aren't all under identical tenure — we can't be certain which from public records alone. Confirm the exact lot/parcel status for the specific unit you're considering before relying on this page.

Pad rent has run around $550/month, including water, garbage, recycling, and snow removal. Pets are allowed — up to two, with a height limit around 15-17 inches at the shoulder and no vicious breeds. Rentals are not permitted.

This page provides general information based on publicly available land registry records, MLS listing data, and our own research. It is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.

Nancee Way Village — FAQ

Is Nancee Way Village on Westbank First Nation land?
Predominantly yes — most records confirm WFN land with two headleases running to 2109 and 2118. We've seen one listing suggest a specific unit may differ, so confirm the exact parcel for any specific home before relying on this.
What's pad rent at Nancee Way Village?
Recent pad rent has run around $550/month, including water, garbage, recycling, and snow removal.
Can I rent out my home at Nancee Way Village?
No, rentals are not permitted at this park.
Is Nancee Way Village age-restricted?
No — it's an all-ages, family-oriented community, not a 55+ park.
