McDougall Creek Estates

1929 Highway 97 South, West Kelowna — 100-unit 45+ community on Westbank First Nation land

Westbank First Nation Land

McDougall Creek Estates is a 100-unit manufactured home community at 1929 Highway 97 South in West Kelowna, on Westbank First Nation land. The age restriction here is 45+ — not 55+, which is sometimes assumed for Okanagan manufactured home parks generally.

The headlease runs to December 19, 2054. Pets are allowed — one dog or two cats — and rentals are permitted if approved by the park manager first.

As with Jubilee, financing at McDougall Creek Estates specifically is limited to a short list of lenders: BMO and Peace Hills Trust are the ones willing to finance homes here. Confirm this with your mortgage broker before writing an offer — see our financing guide for the broader picture on why this happens.

This page provides general information only and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.
