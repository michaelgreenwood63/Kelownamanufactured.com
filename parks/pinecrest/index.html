Pinecrest MHP

12022 Pretty Road, Lake Country

BC Land — Confirmed

The short version: Pinecrest MHP is a manufactured home community in Lake Country at 12022 Pretty Road. This park sits on regular BC provincial land, not a reserve — unlike several of its Lake Country neighbours on Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7.

A note on the address

720 Commonwealth Road, an address sometimes mistakenly attributed to this park, actually belongs to Meadowbrook Estates, a separate, nearby park. Pinecrest MHP's real address is 12022 Pretty Road — if you've seen conflicting addresses for this park elsewhere, that's why.

What we know

Because Pinecrest sits on ordinary BC land, it falls under the province's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rather than any First Nation's own framework — see our full jurisdiction breakdown for what that means in practice. Pad rent generally runs $694 to $765 a month, depending on the specific unit and any updates made to it.

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. Park policies, pad rent, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.
