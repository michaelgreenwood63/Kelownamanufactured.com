Green Acres

3225 Shannon Lake Road, West Kelowna — 69 units on Westbank First Nation land

Westbank First Nation Land

Green Acres is a 69-unit manufactured home community at 3225 Shannon Lake Road in West Kelowna. More than one MLS listing for this park explicitly states "Leasehold, Indigenous (WFN) lands" in the listing remarks — a direct, named confirmation that this is Westbank First Nation land, not just a general reserve designation.

The headlease runs to December 31, 2094 — a long remaining term, well into the next century. Recent pad rent has run roughly $590-$626/month, including water and sewer.

Pets are generally permitted, up to two dogs. One thing to know before buying with rental income in mind: rentals at Green Acres are restricted to immediate family members only — this isn't a park where you can rent to the general public.

This page provides general information based on publicly available land registry records, MLS listing data, and our own research. It is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.
