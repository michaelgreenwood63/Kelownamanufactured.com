Shady Rest

2095 Boucherie Road, West Kelowna — 49 units on Westbank First Nation land.

WFN Land

The short version: Shady Rest is a 49-unit community on the Boucherie Road corridor, on Westbank First Nation land under a headlease running to August 2105.

Pets and rentals

No dogs are permitted. Indoor cats only are allowed. Rentals are not allowed.

Lease term

The WFN headlease for Shady Rest runs to August 31, 2105.

Land status

Shady Rest sits on Westbank First Nation land — WFN's Residential Premises Law governs, not BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. See our full breakdown.
