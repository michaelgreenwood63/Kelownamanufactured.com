The Edgewater Pines

6663 Highway 97 South, Peachland — also known as Edgewater Estates in some listings.

The short version: The Edgewater Pines — also referred to as Edgewater Estates — is an all-ages manufactured home community on Highway 97 South in Peachland. Our data on this park is thinner than most in our directory; here's what we can confirm.

Land & Jurisdiction

Based on its location and the pattern across Peachland's other confirmed parks, we treat The Edgewater Pines as regular BC provincial/municipal land rather than reserve land, though we don't have the same direct MLS legal-description confirmation for this specific park that we have for others in our directory. See our full land jurisdiction guide for context, and we'd confirm this directly before relying on it for a specific transaction.
