How to read the badges: WFN Land means Westbank First Nation's Residential Premises Law applies, not BC's provincial Act. Duck Lake IR#7 marks Okanagan Indian Band reserve land in Lake Country — a separate framework from WFN, details still being confirmed. BC / Freehold means regular provincial law applies, or in rare cases you'd own the land outright. See our full explainer for what each actually means.
