Deer Meadows Estates

8900 Jim Bailey Road, Lake Country (East/Oyama)

Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7

The short version: Deer Meadows Estates is an actively-selling, new-construction manufactured home community in Lake Country's East/Oyama area, built by Crafted Development Corp (homes by Woodland Crafted Homes) with an on-site sales team and presentation centre.

Whose land is this on?

About Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7: this park sits on reserve land administered by the Okanagan Indian Band — a completely different First Nation and reserve from Westbank First Nation in West Kelowna. We have not found evidence the Okanagan Indian Band has passed its own dedicated residential tenancy law the way WFN has; protections here likely depend more heavily on the specific lease or sublease document and general Indian Act leasing provisions. Confirm directly with the Okanagan Indian Band's Lands Department or a lawyer before relying on any assumption. See our full explanation.

An unusual feature: short-term rentals are explicitly permitted

Almost every park in our directory — WFN, Duck Lake IR#7, or regular BC land — explicitly forbids rentals or restricts them heavily. Deer Meadows Estates is a real exception: daily and weekly rentals are permitted. If short-term rental income is part of your plan, this is one of the few communities in this market where that's actually allowed — worth confirming current terms directly, since rules like this can change.

Pad rent, age restriction, pets

This is an all-ages community (no age restriction). Pad rent generally runs $475 to $622 a month, with most homes clustering around $575-$600. Units here are newer builds, some from as recently as 2025-2026, which shows in home sizes from a compact 750 sq ft up to around 1,470 sq ft. Pet policy: up to 2 small dogs (≤50 lbs and/or 24" at the shoulder when mature) or 2 indoor cats, max 2 animals per household.

New construction here is still active — if you're interested in a newly-built unit rather than resale, this park's developer sales team is a direct point of contact, separate from general resale listings.

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. Park policies, pad rent, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.

Deer Meadows Estates — FAQ

Is Deer Meadows Estates on Westbank First Nation land?
No. It sits on Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7, administered by the Okanagan Indian Band — a different First Nation and reserve from Westbank First Nation in West Kelowna.
Can I rent out my home at Deer Meadows Estates?
Yes — daily and weekly rentals are permitted, unusual for this market, where most parks forbid short-term rentals entirely. Confirm current rules before relying on this for a specific unit.
Is this park age-restricted?
No, Deer Meadows Estates is all-ages.
