Park Lake Estates

3270 Shannon Lake Road, West Kelowna — 37 units on Westbank First Nation land

Westbank First Nation Land

Park Lake Estates is a 37-unit manufactured home community at 3270 Shannon Lake Road in West Kelowna, on Westbank First Nation land, with a headlease running to July 8, 2062.

Pad rent has run around $503/month. Pets require park approval, and rentals are not permitted at this community.

This page provides general information only and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.
