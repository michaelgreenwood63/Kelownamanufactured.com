Crystal Springs

1850 Shannon Lake Road, West Kelowna

BC Land — Confirmed

The short version: Crystal Springs is a 55+, gated manufactured home community of 200+ homes in West Kelowna's Shannon Lake area. Unlike many of its Boucherie Road neighbours, it sits on regular BC provincial/municipal land, not Westbank First Nation land — confirmed independently across four separate MLS listings, each explicitly marking "Indigenous Lnd: No" with zoning code RMP (Residential Mobile Park, a standard West Kelowna municipal designation).

What that means for you

Because Crystal Springs sits on ordinary provincial land, it falls under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the same framework that governs pad-rental communities across the rest of the province. Annual pad rent increases are capped by a provincial formula, and selling your home means assigning the site tenancy agreement to the buyer through the standard RTB-10 process, with the park required to respond to a consent request within a set window. See our full breakdown of land jurisdiction for how this compares to the WFN-leasehold parks nearby.

Pad rent, pets, and who can live there

Pad rent has shown up on recent listings anywhere from about $518 to $700 a month — several listings note that rent jumps for a new owner after a sale closes, so don't assume the current owner's rate carries forward. The community is 55+, gated, with a clubhouse and other shared amenities. Pet policy allows one cat or dog, generally capped around 12–14 inches at the shoulder — worth confirming the exact current rule with the park directly, since we've seen slightly different numbers cited across listings.

Why buyers like it: Crystal Springs is one of the more established, amenity-rich 55+ parks in West Kelowna, and because it's on regular BC land, financing tends to be more straightforward than parks where only one or two specific lenders will even consider the property.
