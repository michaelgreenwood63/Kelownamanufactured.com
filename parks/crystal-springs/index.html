Crystal Springs

1850 Shannon Lake Road, West Kelowna

BC Land — Confirmed

The short version: Crystal Springs is a 55+, gated manufactured home community of 200+ homes in West Kelowna's Shannon Lake area, on regular BC provincial/municipal land, zoned RMP (Residential Mobile Park) by West Kelowna.

What that means for you

Crystal Springs falls under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Annual pad rent increases are capped by a provincial formula, and selling a home means assigning the site tenancy agreement to the buyer through the standard RTB-10 process. See our full breakdown of land jurisdiction.

Pad rent, pets, and who can live there

Pad rent generally runs $518 to $700 a month; the rate can change for a new owner after a sale closes. The community is 55+, gated, with a clubhouse and other shared amenities. Pet policy allows one cat or dog, generally under 12–14 inches at the shoulder.

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. Park policies, pad rent, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.

Common Questions

Crystal Springs — FAQ

Is Crystal Springs on Westbank First Nation land?
No. Crystal Springs sits on regular BC provincial land, zoned RMP (Residential Mobile Park) by West Kelowna. It's governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not WFN's Residential Premises Law.
What's the pad rent at Crystal Springs?
Pad rent generally runs $518-$700 a month, and the rate can increase for a new owner after closing. Confirm the current rate for a specific unit before making an offer.
Is Crystal Springs age-restricted?
Yes, it's a 55+ community.
What's the pet policy?
One cat or dog, generally under 12-14 inches at the shoulder — confirm the exact current rule with park management.
