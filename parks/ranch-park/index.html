Ranch Park

3591 Old Vernon Road, Ellison — a 55+ manufactured home community on regular BC land, north of central Kelowna.

The short version: Ranch Park is a 55+ manufactured home community in the Ellison area north of central Kelowna, on regular BC provincial land, with pad rent among the higher end of what we've found across the region — roughly $790 to $797 a month.

Land & Jurisdiction

Ranch Park sits on regular BC provincial/municipal land, confirmed directly via MLS legal description data — not a First Nations reserve. BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies as normal. See our full land jurisdiction guide for how this compares to the reserve-land parks in West Kelowna and Lake Country.

Age Restriction, Pad Rent & Pets

Ranch Park is a 55+ community. Pad rent runs roughly $790 to $797 a month — on the higher side compared to most parks in our directory, worth factoring into your budget alongside the home's purchase price. Pets are allowed: up to 2 dogs, 2 cats, or one of each, with dogs restricted to a maximum of 14 inches at the shoulder.

This page provides general information based on publicly available land registry and MLS data, and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet, and rental policies can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park before relying on them.
