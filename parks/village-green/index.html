Village Green

3381 Village Green Way, West Kelowna — 81 units on Westbank First Nation land, managed by Compass Communities.

WFN Land

The short version: Village Green is an 81-unit all-ages community managed by Compass Communities, on Westbank First Nation land. One thing sets it apart from most other WFN parks we've mapped: its headlease runs only to July 2036 — noticeably sooner than the 2080s-2150s terms typical elsewhere in this market.

Pad rent and fees

Pad rent runs around $650/month as of recent listings. New buyers also pay a $250 application fee.

Pets and rentals

Dogs are allowed, but any dog exceeding 30lbs when fully grown is not permitted. Rentals are allowed, though subject to some restrictions.

A nearer-term lease worth understanding

Village Green's Westbank First Nation headlease expires in July 2036 — meaningfully sooner than most parks in our directory. As our land-jurisdiction guide explains, a shorter remaining lease term doesn't automatically mean anything specific happens to existing homeowners, but it's a genuinely relevant fact to understand and ask about directly — both for your own planning and because it can affect financing options. We'd recommend asking the park management directly about renewal plans before buying here.

This page provides general information based on publicly available sources, land registry records, and current MLS listing data. It is not legal advice. Pad rent, policies, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.
