Lakecity Mobile Home Park

3535 McCulloch Road, SE Kelowna — one of only two manufactured home parks we've confirmed in South East Kelowna.

The short version: Lakecity Mobile Home Park is a 55+ community on McCulloch Road in SE Kelowna, near Gallagher's Canyon Golf & Country Club. It's one of only two manufactured home parks we've confirmed in this specific part of Kelowna — most of the market is concentrated in West Kelowna and Lake Country, so SE Kelowna options are genuinely limited.

Land & Jurisdiction

Lakecity sits on regular BC provincial/municipal land — not a First Nations reserve. That means BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies in the normal way: capped annual pad rent increases, and selling a home here means assigning the site tenancy using the standard RTB-10 process. See our full land jurisdiction guide for how this compares to the West Kelowna and Lake Country reserve-land parks.

Age Restriction & Pets

Lakecity is a 55+ community. Its pet policy is unusually strict for this market — no pets are allowed at all, not even with park approval. If a pet is part of your household, this isn't the right community; most other parks in our directory allow at least one approved pet.

What we don't yet have

Our data on Lakecity is thinner than most parks in our directory — we don't yet have a confirmed unit count or current pad rent figure. We'll update this page as we confirm more directly with the park.

No. Unlike most parks in our directory, Lakecity does not allow pets at all, with no exceptions we're aware of.
Is Lakecity on reserve land?
No — it's on regular BC provincial/municipal land, so BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies, not a First Nation's own framework.
