Pine Hills Mobile Home Park

5371 Princeton Avenue, Peachland — a 55+ community with one of the strictest pet policies we've found anywhere in our directory.

The short version: Pine Hills is a 55+ manufactured home community on Princeton Avenue in Peachland, on regular BC provincial land, with pad rent around $700 a month and a notably strict pet policy worth knowing before you fall in love with a listing.

Land & Jurisdiction

Pine Hills sits on regular BC provincial/municipal land, not a First Nations reserve. BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies as normal — see our full land jurisdiction guide for context.

Age Restriction & Pad Rent

Pine Hills is a 55+ community with pad rent around $700 a month.

Pet Policy — unusually strict

Worth stating plainly: Pine Hills has one of the strictest pet policies in our entire directory. Dogs must be no taller than roughly 6 inches (15cm) at the shoulder — a genuinely small-breed-only restriction — or be a certified service dog. If you have a mid-size or larger dog, this isn't the right community; most other parks in our directory are considerably more permissive.

This page provides general information based on publicly available land registry and MLS data, and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet, and rental policies can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park before relying on them.

Common Questions

Pine Hills Mobile Home Park — FAQ

Can I bring my dog to Pine Hills Mobile Home Park?
Only if it's no taller than about 6 inches (15cm) at the shoulder, or a certified service dog — one of the strictest pet policies we've found in this market. Most other parks allow considerably larger dogs.
Is Pine Hills age-restricted?
Yes — it's a 55+ community.
How much is pad rent at Pine Hills?
Around $700 a month.
Is Pine Hills on reserve land?
No — it's on regular BC provincial/municipal land, so BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies.
