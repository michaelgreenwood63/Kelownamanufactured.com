The Vintage

2026 Boucherie Road, West Kelowna — a newer 45+ community on Westbank First Nation land.

WFN Land

The short version: The Vintage is a newer, master-planned 45+ community on Westbank First Nation land — 99 lots, and still actively being built out as recently as 2024, which gives it a different feel from the older, long-established WFN parks nearby.

Pad rent

Pad rent is confirmed at $537.08/month as of a recent listing — on the lower end compared to other WFN parks in the immediate area.

Lease term

The Vintage operates under a 125-year Westbank First Nation headlease running from July 15, 2020 to July 14, 2145. The operator, The Vintage at Westbank Limited Partnership, paid $2,000,000 upfront as the entire rent for the full term rather than ongoing payments. The managing general partner is Sage Creek Developments Inc — the same company that operates the nearby Sage Creek community.

Age restriction

45+.

Still actively developing

Unlike most of the WFN parks we've mapped, The Vintage had units still being newly built as of 2024 — worth knowing if you're comparing an established resale unit against a newer build within the same community.

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. Pad rent, policies, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.
