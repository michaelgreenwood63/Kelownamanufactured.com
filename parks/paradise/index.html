Paradise

2035 Boucherie Road, West Kelowna — 122 units on Westbank First Nation land, with lake access

Westbank First Nation Land

Paradise is a 122-unit manufactured home community at 2035 Boucherie Road in West Kelowna, on Westbank First Nation land, with a headlease running to May 19, 2104. It sits along the West Kelowna Wine Trail with direct access to Okanagan Lake and a private beach — a genuine standout feature compared to most parks in this market.

The park's standard pet policy allows cats only. The waterfront section, marketed under the name "Paradise Beach MHP," enforces a stricter no-dogs rule. Rentals are not permitted anywhere in the park.

Pad rent varies by location within the park — standard units have run lower, while premium waterfront pads have been seen around $723-$750/month, a real premium over the park's typical rate.

This page provides general information only and is not legal advice. Pad rent, pet policy, and other park rules can change — please confirm current terms directly with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on this page.

Common Questions

Paradise — FAQ

Does Paradise have lake access?
Yes — Paradise is located along the West Kelowna Wine Trail with direct Okanagan Lake access and a private beach, unusual among the parks we cover.
What's the pet policy at Paradise?
Cats only on standard lots. At least one waterfront unit (marketed as "Paradise Beach MHP") showed a stricter no-dogs rule — confirm the specific unit's policy.
Can I rent out my home at Paradise?
No, rentals are not permitted.
Is "Paradise Beach MHP" a different park from Paradise?
It appears to be the same park's waterfront/beach section rather than a separate community, based on the shared address — but we'd confirm this directly before assuming identical rules apply.
