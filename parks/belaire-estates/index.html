Belaire Estates

9020 Jim Bailey Road, Lake Country (East/Oyama)

Duck Lake IR#7 (predominantly)

The short version: Belaire Estates sits on the same Jim Bailey Road corridor as Deer Meadows Estates, predominantly on Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7 land. At least one unit here sits on a different, non-reserve parcel instead — so while the park is predominantly on reserve land, that isn't necessarily true for every lot. Confirm the specific parcel for any unit you're considering.

Whose land is this on?

About Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7: this park sits on reserve land administered by the Okanagan Indian Band — a completely different First Nation and reserve from Westbank First Nation in West Kelowna. We have not found evidence the Okanagan Indian Band has passed its own dedicated residential tenancy law the way WFN has; protections here likely depend more heavily on the specific lease or sublease document and general Indian Act leasing provisions. Confirm directly with the Okanagan Indian Band's Lands Department or a lawyer before relying on any assumption. See our full explanation.

For Belaire Estates specifically: most of the park sits on Duck Lake IR#7 land, with a headlease to August 31, 2114 — a long, comfortable term, not a near-term risk like nearby Meadowbrook Estates. The parcel that differs may reflect a genuine mixed-parcel situation, similar to what we've seen at Sierra's Westside and Nancee Way Village in West Kelowna.

Pad rent and pets

Belaire Estates has the highest pad rent in our entire directory — generally $745 to $850 a month. Pet policy: one pet per household, subject to park approval.

Before you make an offer here: given the mixed signals on land status, it's worth specifically asking the park or a lawyer to confirm the land status for the exact lot you're looking at, rather than assuming it matches the park's general pattern.

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. Park policies, pad rent, and lease terms can change — please confirm current details with the park and a lawyer or notary before relying on them.
