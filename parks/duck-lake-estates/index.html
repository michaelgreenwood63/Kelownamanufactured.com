Duck Lake Estates

8945 Highway 97 North, Lake Country (East/Oyama)

Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7

The short version: this community goes by two names depending on the listing — "Duck Lake Estates" on some MLS sheets, "Creek Run Developments" on others — but the legal description consistently refers to it as "Creek Run IR7," confirming it's the same physical park at the same address. Don't be thrown off if you see both names; they refer to the same community.

Whose land is this on?

About Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7: this park sits on reserve land administered by the Okanagan Indian Band — a completely different First Nation and reserve from Westbank First Nation in West Kelowna. We have not found evidence the Okanagan Indian Band has passed its own dedicated residential tenancy law the way WFN has; protections here likely depend more heavily on the specific lease or sublease document and general Indian Act leasing provisions. Confirm directly with the Okanagan Indian Band's Lands Department or a lawyer before relying on any assumption. See our full explanation.

What we know

This is an all-ages community. Pad rent across multiple listings runs roughly $500-$515 a month. We have less detailed pet/rental-policy data for this specific park than for its larger neighbour Deer Meadows Estates — confirm current park rules directly before making an offer.

A note on naming: when researching this park elsewhere (with the park itself, a lender, or your lawyer), it's worth using both names — "Duck Lake Estates" and "Creek Run" — since records and local knowledge may be split between them.
