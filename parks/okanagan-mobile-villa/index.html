Okanagan Mobile Villa

1132 Findlay Road, Rutland North — a separate community from Okanagan Villa Manufactured Home Park, despite the near-identical name.

The short version: Okanagan Mobile Villa is on Findlay Road in Rutland North, on regular BC provincial land, with pad rent around $625 a month.

Don't confuse this with Okanagan Villa. There are two separately named, separately located parks with very similar names in the Rutland North area: Okanagan Mobile Villa (this page, 1132 Findlay Road) and Okanagan Villa Manufactured Home Park (670 McCurdy Road). They are not the same community — double-check the address before making an offer or contacting a park manager.

Land & Jurisdiction

Okanagan Mobile Villa sits on regular BC provincial/municipal land, not a First Nations reserve. BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies as normal — see our full land jurisdiction guide for context.

Pad Rent

Pad rent runs around $625 a month.

Is Okanagan Mobile Villa the same as Okanagan Villa?

How much is pad rent at Okanagan Mobile Villa?
Around $625 a month.
Is Okanagan Mobile Villa on reserve land?
