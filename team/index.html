Kelowna Manufactured / Our Team

Mark Jontz

3,000+ career Okanagan real estate transactions — now digging into one of the most overlooked corners of this market: manufactured and mobile home parks, reserve land included.

Most agents treat a manufactured home listing like any other — list it, hold an open house, write an offer. That approach misses almost everything that actually matters in this market: whose land the park sits on, whether a lender will even touch the deal, what the park's own approval process requires before an offer can go anywhere, and what "selling" actually involves when the home itself is personal property but the pad underneath it isn't yours at all.

Why this niche, specifically

There are only so many manufactured home parks in the Central Okanagan — we've mapped close to fifty of them, across West Kelowna, Lake Country, Kelowna proper, and Peachland. A meaningful share sit on reserve land, governed by frameworks most agents never dig into at all. That's exactly the gap this site and Mark's focus are built around: real, park-specific knowledge instead of generic advice that happens to also apply to a regular detached home.

"You want to move out of your own volition." That's the principle Mark's built his broader practice on for decades — and here, it means giving buyers and sellers the real picture on financing, jurisdiction, and park rules before they're committed to a deal, not after.

What working with Mark actually looks like

It starts with figuring out whose land a park actually sits on — Westbank First Nation, Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7, regular BC provincial land, or in rare cases land you'd own outright — because that single fact changes everything downstream: whether financing is realistic, what the assignment process involves, and what questions actually matter before you make an offer or accept one.
