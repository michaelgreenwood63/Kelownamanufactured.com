Our Team

Mark Jontz & Associates, Royal LePage Kelowna

Mark Jontz

Mark Jontz has spent 25+ years in Okanagan real estate with Royal LePage Kelowna, where he was the first inductee into the brokerage's Chairman's Hall of Fame. Across his career he's handled thousands of transactions throughout the Central Okanagan — Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, and Peachland.

KelownaManufactured.com is a focused effort to bring that same depth of local knowledge to one of the most overlooked corners of this market: manufactured and mobile home communities, including the Westbank First Nation and Duck Lake Indian Reserve No. 7 leasehold parks that most agents never dig into. That means knowing which land jurisdiction applies to a specific park, which lenders will actually finance a home there, and what a sale genuinely involves before a client ever makes an offer.

Mark works directly alongside each park's management, lawyers and notaries handling the legal side, and — where reserve land is involved — the relevant First Nation's own processes, to make sure nothing about a transaction is assumed rather than confirmed.

Part of the broader Mark Jontz & Associates network, which also includes MarkJontz.com, KelownaListings.com, KelownaDownsizing.com, and InCareOfKelowna.com.
